Screener Settings

Everything runs in your browser. Your API key and the loaded data are stored only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to any server except Financial Modeling Prep.

Don't have one? Get a free key at financialmodelingprep.com. The free tier allows 250 requests per day.

Each symbol uses 4 API requests (quote, balance sheet, financial growth, analyst estimates). The free tier allows 250 requests per day, so the default of 60 keeps a single run under that limit. Already-loaded symbols are skipped, so you can resume on another day to fill in the rest of the 100.

How stocks are scored

Each company is graded on seven factors drawn from the methodology. A factor scores 2 (strong), 1 (acceptable), or 0 (weak). The score is the percentage of available points earned, and the verdict follows from it.

  • Revenue Growth TTM & FWD — strong above 15%, acceptable 8–15%.
  • EPS Growth TTM & FWD — strong above 15%, acceptable when positive.
  • P/E FWD vs EPS Growth — strong when P/E FWD is below the forward EPS growth rate (the primary signal).
  • PEG FWD — strong below 1.0, acceptable up to 2.0.
  • Cash vs Debt — strong when cash exceeds debt, acceptable when debt is under 3× cash.

Verdict: Pass = score 80%+, Watch = 60–79%, Fail = under 60%. Verdicts reflect fundamentals only; entry timing (RSI, 52-week range) is a separate step covered in the methodology.
