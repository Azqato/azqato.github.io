Free tools that just work.

Fourteen utilities for writing, formatting, converting and keeping track of things. Ten run entirely in this browser: no accounts, no uploads, and no server that ever sees what you typed.

No sign-up No servers Open & private Works offline*

Why these tools?

Most online utilities upload your text, links and files to a server you can't see. These don't. Every tool hosted here is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that executes entirely on your device, so your data never leaves the browser tab.

That also means they're fast, free forever, and keep working with the network off.

*One exception, stated plainly: the Favicon Downloader has to ask a public icon service for the images, so it needs a connection and that one lookup leaves your device. Every other hosted tool works offline.
