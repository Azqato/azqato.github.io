Password Generator

Generate a strong random password. The randomness comes from your operating system, not from a server, and the result is never transmitted.

Include

Strength is the entropy of the generator, not a judgement of the string: it is the right number for a password this tool produced and the wrong number for one a person chose. Turning off look-alike characters removes I l 1 O 0 o, which makes a password easier to read aloud and slightly weaker for its length.
